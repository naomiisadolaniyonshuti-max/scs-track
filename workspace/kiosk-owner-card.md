# Kiosk Back Office -- Owner's Card (KIOSK_BACK_OFFICE-v2)

## What This Sheet Does
- **LIST**: Holds your items (`Sugar`, `Bread`, `Milk`, `Rice`, `Cooking oil`), current selling prices, and reorder levels.
- **RECORD**: Holds every stock delivery (`IN`) and sale (`OUT`), one row per event.
- **DASHBOARD**: Shows today's 5 numbers automatically (`Sales This Month`, `Units Sold`, `Units in Stock`, `Items to Reorder`, and `Items Out of Stock`).

## Daily Routine (5 Minutes)
1. Open **RECORD** on your phone or laptop.
2. Go to the next blank row at the bottom—never overwrite an old row.
3. Enter **Date**, pick **Item** from the list, pick **Direction** (`IN` for stock bought, `OUT` for sales), enter **Quantity**, and enter the **Price** charged that day.
4. Check **DASHBOARD** to see any item flagged **REORDER**.

## Fixing a Mistake
- Never delete or edit an old row on **RECORD**.
- Add a new correcting row at the bottom with today's date, a note, and the adjusting quantity so the ledger history stays honest.

## Rules & Who to Call
- Do not type on **DASHBOARD** (all cells are formulas).
- Only change prices on **LIST** when a price changes going forward; past rows on **RECORD** keep their original price.
