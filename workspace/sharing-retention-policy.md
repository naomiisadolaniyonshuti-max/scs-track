## 1. Where files live
All active business records (customer lists, price sheets, and operations logs) live in the company Shared Drive rather than individual My Drive folders, so files stay with the business when staff roles change. Personal My Drive is restricted to temporary working drafts only.

## 2. Sharing outside the business
External sharing is set to warn when files are shared outside the organization, and public "Anyone with the link" sharing is turned off for the practice OU. External partners receive view or comment access to specific files only, never manager access to a Shared Drive.

## 3. Who can sign in
Only active staff accounts in the practice Organizational Unit may sign in. All staff and admin accounts must enroll in 2-Step Verification (authenticator app or prompt), and mobile devices accessing work data must enforce a screen lock.

## 4. What we keep, how long, where
Active operational spreadsheets and financial records stay in the Shared Drive for the current trading year plus a retention period to be checked with the business owner's accountant. Deleted Drive files remain in Drive trash for 30 days before permanent deletion, with an additional 25-day admin restore window.

## 5. The monthly export
On the first Monday of each month, the designated admin exports a full user directory CSV and runs a Google Takeout or Shared Drive archive of core business folders to encrypted offline storage. Each quarterly backup drill includes restoring one test file to confirm readability.

## 6. Who holds admin
One primary owner holds the Super Admin role, with one designated backup admin holding a scoped role for password resets and user management. Admin accounts are never used for daily email or web browsing, and backup codes are stored offline on paper.

## 7. When someone leaves
On a leaver's final day, the admin suspends the account immediately, resets sign-in cookies, and revokes app passwords. Within 24 hours, the admin transfers Drive file ownership to the department manager and archives essential records before deleting or reassigning the license after 30 days.
